Appendix G — The newest patients in detail

The newest patients, from Nova 588 to Nova 632, treated exactly like every other patient: each scan is put through the rule of Appendix C (water peak height ≥ 0.35 and water centred at q ≥ 19), without looking at the labels, most of which are not in yet. Nova 588-595 come from the main archive and Nova 596 onwards from the scans added on 9 November 2026 (measured 2-6 November), prepared in exactly the same way (Figure A.1). Of their 265 scans, 52 (20%) are usable, and 11 of the 45 patients have both breasts usable. Most labels are not in yet; once they are, the patients with both breasts usable can be used to test the comparison of Appendix C (Chapter 7).
